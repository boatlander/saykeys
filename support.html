SayKeys Support

SayKeys gives you a voice when you can't speak: type a few letters, tap Speak, and your iPhone or iPad says it out loud. Questions? Email travisboatman@carbonated.com.

Which voices can I use?

Two great options. Use the free built-in Apple voices, which are ready right away and work offline. Or connect your own ElevenLabs account for lifelike ElevenLabs voices: on a blank message, tap the menu icon in the top-right corner, choose Connect an external voice, paste your ElevenLabs API key and pick a voice. With ElevenLabs connected, the text you speak is sent to ElevenLabs to create the audio. "Remove connected voice" switches back to the Apple voice.

How do I get a better Apple voice?

For the most natural built-in sound, go to Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices on your iPhone or iPad, then download an Enhanced or Premium voice. SayKeys uses it automatically.

Does it need internet?

No, unless you connect an external voice. Then Speak needs internet; without it SayKeys uses the Apple voice automatically. With the built-in voices, everything runs on your iPhone or iPad, even with no signal, so you can use it in a hospital room or anywhere else.

Where's my data?

Only on your iPhone or iPad, unless you connect an external voice, which sends the text you speak to ElevenLabs. No account, no ads, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

What do I need?

iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. iPhone or iPad. English only for now.

Good to know

SayKeys is a communication tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. Talk with your doctor or speech-language pathologist about the best ways for you to communicate.
