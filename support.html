SayKeys Support

SayKeys gives you a voice when you can't speak: type a few letters, tap Speak, and your iPhone says it out loud. Questions? Email travisboatman@carbonated.com.

How do I get a better voice?

For the most natural sound, go to Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices on your iPhone, then download an Enhanced or Premium voice. SayKeys uses it automatically.

Does it need internet?

No. Everything runs on your iPhone, even with no signal, so you can use it in a hospital room or anywhere else.

Where's my data?

Only on your phone. What you type never leaves it. No account, no ads, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

What do I need?

iOS 18 or later. iPhone only. English only for now.

Good to know

SayKeys is a communication tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or cure any condition. Talk with your doctor or speech-language pathologist about the best ways for you to communicate.
