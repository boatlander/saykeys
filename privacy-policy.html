SayKeys Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

SayKeys is made by Travis Boatman.

What stays on your iPhone or iPad. Everything you type is processed on your iPhone or iPad. The words SayKeys learns to suggest, and any family words, stay on your device. With the built-in Apple voices, SayKeys works without an internet connection and sends nothing to us or anyone else.

Optional external voice (ElevenLabs). If you choose Menu > Connect an external voice and enter your own ElevenLabs API key, then each time you tap Speak or tap a word to hear it, that text is sent over an encrypted connection directly from your iPhone or iPad to ElevenLabs to create the audio. Your API key is also sent so ElevenLabs can authenticate your account. This happens under your own ElevenLabs account and is covered by ElevenLabs' terms and privacy policy (https://elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy). ElevenLabs may keep your requests, for example in your account's history. SayKeys never receives or stores this text on any server. Your API key is stored only in the Keychain on your iPhone or iPad. "Remove connected voice" deletes it, and SayKeys goes back to the Apple voice.

We have no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. If you delete the app, its data on your device is deleted too.

If you email us for support, we use your email only to reply.
