SayKeys Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 Oct 2026

Effective 4 Oct 2026. SayKeys is made by Travis Boatman.

SayKeys does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you type is processed on your iPhone and stays there. That includes the words the app learns to suggest and any family words you add. Speech is produced by Apple's voices on your device. The app works without an internet connection and sends nothing to us or anyone else.

We have no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. If you delete the app, its data on your phone is deleted too.

If you email us for support, we use your email only to reply.
